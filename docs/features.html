Features

A small program with one job: making several Roblox accounts painless to run.

True multi-client

Roblox refuses to start a second client by using two Windows objects as a lock. MultiRoblox holds both, so every client believes it is the only one.

  • Nothing is injected into Roblox and no Roblox memory is read.
  • Only affects clients opened after MultiRoblox, while it is running.
  • The panel shows MULTI ON or WAITING.

Sign in with your browser

Adding an account opens your default browser on roblox.com. You sign in with the normal captcha and 2FA, and the account appears in the list.

  • Works with Firefox, Edge, Chrome, Brave and Vivaldi.
  • A temporary, empty browser profile is used and deleted afterwards.
  • No password is stored: only the session cookie, encrypted with Windows DPAPI.

Launch many at once

Select several accounts (Ctrl or Shift) and press Play.

  • Opens the clients one by one, with a short pause between them.
  • Waits for each Roblox client to actually appear before moving on.
  • Flags the accounts that failed and tells you how many opened.

Nicknames and ordering

Give every account a name you recognize and put them in the order you like.

  • Double-click an account and type its nickname right on the row.
  • Drag and drop, or use Alt+↑/↓ (moves all selected).
  • The order is saved and also used when tiling windows.

Games by Place ID

Type a Place ID and see the game's name, icon and live player count before you launch.

  • Star a game to add it to your favorites, then switch with the dropdown.
  • The last game you launched is remembered.

Follow a user

Join the server of a friend, or of another account of yours, without hunting for the game first.

  • Pick one of your accounts or type a username or ID.
  • Roblox requires friendship or permission to join.

Online status

See whether each account is offline, online on the website, in Roblox Studio, or in a game (and which one). A colored dot on its profile picture shows it at a glance.

  • Refreshed when the program opens and when you click Status.
  • Each account is checked with its own cookie.
  • Every account shows its Roblox profile picture, cached on your PC.

Tile windows

One click arranges every Roblox client side by side in a grid that fills your screen.

  • Follows your account order.
  • DPI-aware, so sizes come out right on scaled displays.
  • Uses the primary monitor.

Update notice

When a new release is published, an Update button appears in the title bar and opens the download page.

  • Checks on startup and every 6 hours.
  • Nothing is downloaded or installed automatically.
  • Can be turned off from the tray icon menu.

Password lock

Optionally ask for a password every time MultiRoblox opens, and lock it again whenever you want from the tray menu.

  • Set it from the tray icon menu.
  • It only locks the program window: your accounts stay protected by Windows, not by this password.

Close one client

Right-click an account and choose Close client to shut down just that account's Roblox, without touching the others.

  • Works for the clients MultiRoblox opened.
  • Also has Play, Nickname and Remove.

Clear errors and a log

When something fails, each account says why: expired login, rate limit, locked account or no connection.

  • An Open log button shows the details.
  • The log never contains your cookie.

Small things that matter

Login check on startup

Accounts whose login expired are marked in red, so you find out before pressing Play.

Taskbar and tray

Minimizes to the taskbar like any normal app. A tray icon gives quick access to updates, the log and the password lock.

Dark, custom window

A custom title bar and a dark theme that stays consistent, even when the window loses focus.

Tiny and portable

A single file of about 60 KB. No installer, no runtime to download: it uses the .NET Framework that ships with Windows.

Want to try it?

Free and open source. Takes less than a minute.
